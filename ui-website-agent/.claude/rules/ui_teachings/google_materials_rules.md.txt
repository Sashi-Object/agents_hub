# Material Design: Text Legibility & Accessibility Standards

Color plays a vital role in text legibility and user accessibility[cite: 1]. 

---

## 1. Legibility Standards & Accessibility
* **Accessibility Goal:** Ensure diverse users can successfully navigate, understand, and use your user interface[cite: 1].
* **WCAG 2.0 Level AA Compliance:** 
  * Normal text requires a minimum color contrast ratio of **4.5:1** against its background[cite: 1].
  * Large text requires a minimum ratio of **3:1**[cite: 1].

---

## 2. Text Backgrounds

### Text on Colored Backgrounds
* **Theme Matching:** Use black text on light backgrounds, and white text on dark backgrounds[cite: 1]. If supporting both light and dark themes, verify contrast across each theme[cite: 1].
* **Using Text Opacity:** Instead of opaque grey text on colored surfaces, use transparent black or white layers to preserve legibility and pull in background hues[cite: 1].

### Opacity Levels for Dark Text on Light Backgrounds (e.g., #000000 on #FFFFFF)
* **High-emphasis text:** 87% opacity[cite: 1]
* **Medium-emphasis text and hint text:** 60% opacity[cite: 1]
* **Disabled text:** 38% opacity[cite: 1]

### Colored Text and Backgrounds
* Colored text should be used sparingly for headlines, buttons, and links to draw selective attention[cite: 1].
* **Best Practice:** Large headlines and short text snippets are ideal for colored text[cite: 1]. Avoid long colored body copy as it decreases readability[cite: 1].

---

## 3. Helper Text & Text Selection

### Helper Text
* Provides context about a field's input while meeting WCAG standards[cite: 1].
* **Examples on light backgrounds:**
  * **High emphasis helper:** `#000000` at 87% opacity[cite: 1]
  * **Default color helper text:** `#000000` at 60% opacity[cite: 1]
  * **Default error helper text:** `#B00020` at 100% opacity[cite: 1]

### Selected Text
* Text selection can leverage brand accent colors, provided the selected text remains legible against the selection color, and the selection color contrasts with the background[cite: 1].
* Alternative treatments include outlines, motion, or checkmark icons[cite: 1].

---

## 4. Icons and Symbols
While icons do not strictly need to meet WCAG text legibility standards, they should be high-visibility[cite: 1]. Dark icons (`#000000`) on light backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) follow these recommended opacities[cite: 1]:
* **Active icons:** 87% opacity[cite: 1]
* **Inactive icons:** 60% opacity[cite: 1]
* **Disabled icons:** 38% opacity[cite: 1]
